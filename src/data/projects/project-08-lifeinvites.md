    content: "
      <p>LifeInvites reimagines the invitation as a cinematic digital product, not a static card. The platform is built around a simple promise: couples and event hosts describe the celebration they want, then the system helps shape the invitation around their venue, color palette, music, photos, schedule, and guest details. The experience combines a sealed-envelope reveal, motion-led storytelling, live countdowns, venue sections, dress-code guidance, gift notes, and a full RSVP flow into one polished web invitation.</p>
      <p>The public product experience positions LifeInvites as an AI-powered invitation studio. Its workflow is designed to feel concierge-level while remaining automated: a user can begin with a living template, describe the event in natural language, preview the invitation for free, and publish only when ready. Behind the elegant front end is a product model that connects template systems, AI-assisted creative direction, guest data capture, and shareable distribution through link, WhatsApp, or QR.</p>
