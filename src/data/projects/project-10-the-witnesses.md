      <p>The Witnesses is an evangelism app for iOS and Android, built to support believers in the practical work of outreach, prayer, testimony, and local church connection. The public product page positions the app around a clear mission: help Christians go out, praise, pray, preach the Gospel, and document the work with focus and care.</p>
      <p>The product is organized around four core systems: outreach sessions for logging Gospel conversations, prayer and people records for meaningful follow-up, testimonies for community encouragement, and church discovery so local believers can connect outreach activity with discipleship and community. It also includes public App Store readiness pages for privacy, terms, community guidelines, support, account deletion, location use, and user-content expectations.</p>

      <p>The Witnesses turns evangelism from a scattered personal note-taking habit into a structured mobile workflow. The app gives believers a way to remember names, track prayer moments, record follow-ups, share testimonies, and understand nearby mission activity without turning the experience into a social network that distracts from the work.</p>
      <p>For App Store approval and long-term user trust, the project also treats safety and privacy as product features. The policy layer is visible, explicit, and built around sensitive categories: location, community content, account deletion, consent, dignity, and support access.</p>
