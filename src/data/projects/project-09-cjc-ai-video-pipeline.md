    title: "About the project"
    content: "
      <p>This internal video platform was built for CJC Church, an NGO with a recurring need to turn long-form ministry content into short-form media. Instead of treating editing as a fully manual production cycle, the system is designed as an AI-assisted pipeline: upload or register a sermon, process the transcript, identify promising moments, organize short candidates, review clips, and manage the assets that support publishing.</p>
      <p>The application exposes a serious production surface: dashboard, editor, Shorts Studio, sermon records, analytics, connections, review queues, storage controls, and trash/archive management. The architecture points to a full operational workflow, not a single-purpose editor. Source videos, transcripts, rendered clips, storage usage, cloud archiving, YouTube fallback, and version pruning are all treated as first-class parts of the production system.</p>
    "
description2:
    enabled: 1
    title: "Why this platform matters"
    content: "
      <p>For churches, NGOs, and mission-driven organizations, content velocity matters. A sermon or event recording can contain dozens of meaningful moments, but most teams do not have the time to manually search, clip, caption, render, review, and organize every asset. This platform compresses that workflow into a managed pipeline where AI agents support discovery and editing while humans keep final control.</p>
      <p>The result is an internal media command center: one place for long-form source content, short-form candidates, review decisions, rendered output, analytics, and storage discipline. It gives CJC a repeatable system for turning weekly content into a larger digital ministry footprint.</p>
