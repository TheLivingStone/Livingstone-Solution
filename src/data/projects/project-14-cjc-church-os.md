    title: "About the project"
    content: "
      <p>CJC Church is an NGO and faith community in Springfield, Virginia, with a public site built around worship, discipleship, events, giving, sermons, ministries, and service information. Behind that public presence, we built a much deeper internal platform: a full church operating system that helps leadership understand the health of the ministry, support members, manage departments, and keep care from falling through the cracks.</p>
      <p>The system centralizes member profiles, attendance, children’s ministry access, giving records, department-level permissions, and pastoral care workflows. Giving can be attributed across multiple channels — including Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, and other payment methods — so each contribution can connect back to the right member or family profile instead of sitting in disconnected spreadsheets or payment dashboards.</p>
    "
description2:
    enabled: 1
    title: "Why this system matters"
    content: "
      <p>Most churches can see who gave, who attended, or who volunteered only after piecing together fragmented tools. CJC’s operating system brings those signals into one place so leadership can see patterns in real time: who has been missing, who may need follow-up, where attendance is changing, which departments need access, and how children’s ministry can stay organized and accountable.</p>
      <p>The platform is built for ministry care, not just administration. It gives CJC a way to monitor the people entrusted to them, identify gaps before they become silent drop-offs, and equip each department with the access level it needs without exposing the whole system. The result is a modern NGO operations platform that supports both spiritual care and organizational discipline.</p>
