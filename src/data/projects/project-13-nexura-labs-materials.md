      <p>Nexura Labs Materials is a B2B laboratory procurement storefront for research kits, Class-A glassware, safety apparel, measuring supplies, consumables, and laboratory instruments. The platform is built for institutional buyers who need more than a standard ecommerce cart: search, documentation, requisitions, facility accounts, purchase orders, quote requests, and procurement-friendly checkout all sit in the same experience.</p>
      <p>The public site presents a professional supplier workflow with catalog filtering, product documentation search, a requisition cart, net-30 purchase-order logic, institutional billing fields, delivery details, and order authorization. It also positions the company around procurement integrations, corporate pricing grids, batch sizing, invoice reconciliation, logistics, and organized product records.</p>

    title: "Why this platform matters"
    content: "
      <p>Lab procurement is not consumer ecommerce. Buyers need product references, documentation, internal purchasing compatibility, quote workflows, and billing structures that match how academic, pharmaceutical, and research facilities operate. Nexura's platform was designed around those procurement realities rather than forcing institutional users through a generic checkout.</p>
      <p>The result is a storefront that feels credible to technical buyers while still moving them toward action: search the catalog, review documentation, build a requisition, request a quote, register a facility, or submit a purchase order.</p>
